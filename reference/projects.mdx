Projects group studies within your organization.

## list()

```python
client.projects.list() -> ProjectListOut
```

List every project in your organization.

**Returns** [`ProjectListOut`](/reference/models#projectlistout), with `projects` holding a list of [`ProjectOut`](/reference/models#projectout).

```python
for project in client.projects.list().projects:
    print(project.id, project.name, project.study_count)
```
